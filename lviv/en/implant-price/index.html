Dental implant cost: Ukraine vs Poland, Czechia and Germany

If you live abroad and are wondering whether it is worth getting your teeth done back home, the question is usually the same one: will travel and accommodation eat the whole difference. Here are the numbers, not promises.

Updated: 31 July 2026

What an implant with a crown costs

We are comparing like for like: an implant together with its crown, for one tooth. Prices for the implant alone look lower, but that is unfinished work, so comparing those is meaningless.

Why the gap is that big

It is not the material. A MegaGen or Osstem implant costs roughly the same everywhere — it is the same product from the same factory. Almost all of the difference is in what surrounds it: rent, salaries, insurance and administrative overhead. In Lviv those are several times lower than in Munich or Warsaw, while the procedure itself and the manufacturer guarantee are the same.

Does travel cancel out the saving

No, and this is exactly where the arithmetic decides it. Lviv is 70 km from the Polish border. In ONNO’s "Standard" package, the transfer and two nights’ accommodation add €450 to the treatment price. So an implant with a crown, travel and lodging included, comes to from €950.

Even against the cheapest Polish case (€1,400) that still leaves more than €450 per tooth. With more than one tooth, the trip pays for itself on the first.

When it is NOT worth travelling

Honestly: for a single filling or a cleaning the trip does not pay off — getting there costs more than the procedure. It starts to make sense with implants, crowns, veneers, and any work spanning several units. Bear in mind too that implant treatment means at least two trips, with a healing gap between them.

What to check before deciding

  • Prices are approximate. A dentist sets the real figure after an examination — no price list replaces an X-ray.
  • Compare the same line item: implant + crown, not "implants from…".
  • The crown material moves the price: metal-ceramic is cheaper, zirconia costs more and lasts longer.
  • Bone graft or sinus lift is a separate item and not everyone needs it.
  • The guarantee should be written into the treatment plan, not promised verbally.

Common questions

Is dental treatment cheaper in Ukraine than in Poland?

Yes. An implant with a crown starts at €500 in Ukraine against roughly €1,400–2,900 in Poland. The gap holds even after travel and accommodation are added.

What does an implant with a crown cost in Lviv?

From €500 for the treatment alone, or from €950 in the package that includes a transfer and two nights’ accommodation. That is approximate; a dentist confirms the final figure after an exam.

How many trips does implant treatment take?

Usually two: the first for diagnosis and placing the implant, 2–3 days; then 3–6 months of healing at home; the second for the permanent crown, 1–2 days.

Is there a guarantee on the implants?

Yes. MegaGen and Osstem give a lifetime guarantee on the implant itself, conditional on a check-up twice a year, which any dentist abroad can do. Crowns and veneers carry 5–10 years depending on material.
