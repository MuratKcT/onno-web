Guarantee & Timing

Work that is backed, time that is planned

You are travelling from abroad, so two things matter most: how long the result lasts, and how many days it takes.

Lifetime guarantee

on implants

MegaGen and Osstem implants (South Korea) carry a lifetime guarantee. The only condition: a check-up once or twice a year, which can be done remotely or at your dentist abroad.

5–10 years

on crowns and veneers

Ceramic and zirconia work is guaranteed for 5 to 10 years depending on the material. The exact term is written into your treatment plan before anything begins.

First visit

Diagnosis and implant placement

A 3D scan, the treatment plan, then the placement itself: 20 to 40 minutes per implant under local anaesthetic. In many cases you leave with a temporary crown the same day.

2–3 days in Lviv
In between

Healing happens at home

The implant fuses with the bone while you carry on with your normal life abroad. Your coordinator stays in touch and checks in on how healing is going.

3–6 months, no travel
Second visit

The permanent crown

A short trip to fit the permanent crown and check the bite. You go home with the finished result and a written guarantee.

1–2 days in Lviv

Before You Plan Your Trip

Who can travel

Lviv is a border-region city, 70km from Poland, and is generally considered a safer area within Ukraine. Women, children, and men with a legal right to cross the border can use this service without restriction. Men of conscription age should check current exit rules before planning travel — our coordinator can help you confirm the details relevant to your situation.
